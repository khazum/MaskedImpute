# Genome Biology Methodology submission checklist

This checklist is fail-closed: unchecked scientific-evidence or author/legal
items mean the manuscript is not ready for submission. The current manuscript
is a sealed-evidence draft, not a submission-ready article.

## Scientific evidence

- [ ] Fresh development competition covers all four mechanisms, two biological
  draws, paired technical views, every frozen method, and all fixed seeds.
- [ ] All 34 smoke completions are present and reason-coded before the full
  comparator competition begins.
- [ ] The base development competition contains exactly 2,896 scheduled rows:
  16 observed-count rows, 48 capacity-matched autoencoder rows, 1,200
  MaskImpute rows, and 1,632 comparator rows.
- [ ] Comparator readiness retains both controls, all five established methods,
  and at least three modern methods; the execution status of BiAEImpute is
  recorded explicitly rather than inferred.
- [ ] Every eligible method has one complete selected payload and all required
  method bindings; ineligible methods retain complete reason-coded failures.
- [ ] Development selection satisfies the prespecified competitive and safety
  gates without using final data.
- [ ] Method/protocol/runtime/source freeze is sealed.
- [ ] The one-use final claim covers five new biological draws per mechanism,
  paired views, and the complete method/seed denominator.
- [ ] Final execution contains exactly 1,760 scheduled rows, and trajectory
  execution contains exactly 44 trajectory rows.
- [ ] The complete execution-status table retains every scheduled method and
  terminal reason; no numerical or superiority claim is made against
  unavailable methods or against methods lacking the relevant estimand.
- [ ] Final analysis reports median/IQR, individual draws, paired hierarchical
  intervals, Holm families, seed/view variance, W/T/L, probability of
  improvement, Pareto membership, failures, ablations, and scaling.
- [ ] Every manuscript number and figure is generated from the sealed analysis
  artifact; no `PENDING` tokens remain.

## Venue structure and reporting

- [x] Article type is framed as Methodology.
- [ ] Completed same-dataset side-by-side results demonstrate a clear advance
  over current state-of-the-art methods, with known-truth benchmarking where
  possible and demonstrated real-data utility.
- [ ] The final unstructured Abstract remains at most 100 words after the sealed
  result and conclusion replace the draft marker.
- [x] The manuscript supplies 3--10 keywords.
- [x] Main order is Background, Results, Discussion, Conclusions, then Methods.
- [ ] The title page contains every author's full name, institutional address,
  and email address and identifies the corresponding author.
- [ ] All seven required Declarations contain author-approved statements or an
  author-approved `Not applicable` determination.
- [ ] The author-approved AI-use disclosure appears in Methods and accurately
  describes any reportable use and human validation.
- [x] The Abbreviations section matches terms expanded at first use.
- [x] Biological and technical replication are distinguished explicitly.
- [ ] Figure legends give sample size, independent unit, summary/error-bar
  definition, statistical sidedness, and multiplicity family.
- [ ] Minimum Standards of Reporting Checklist is completed against the final
  manuscript.
- [ ] All conclusion-supporting and third-party data are publicly accessible or
  have an ethically or legally necessary restriction stated precisely.
- [ ] In-house scripts and the described software are in a public repository
  under an identified OSI-compliant license.
- [ ] The authors have acted on the journal's recommendation to create a static
  archived release, or have recorded a decision not to do so; any archive that
  is created has a persistent identifier and matches the manuscript release.
- [ ] Genome Biology instructions are rechecked immediately before submission.

## Author, legal, and release input

- [ ] Authors, order, affiliations, correspondence, and ORCID identifiers.
- [ ] CRediT contributions.
- [ ] Funding and grant identifiers, or an approved no-funding statement.
- [ ] Competing-interest declaration.
- [ ] Ethics determination, consent, and consent-for-publication statements.
- [ ] Acknowledgements.
- [ ] Author-approved generative-AI disclosure.
- [ ] An accepted OSI SPDX license for the in-tree project.
- [ ] Public source repository URL, data accessions or reviewer links, and, if
  the authors create the recommended static software archive, its persistent
  identifier.
- [ ] Preprint decision and details, if applicable.

## Package validation

- [x] The vendored class and Vancouver style retain their upstream notices and
  are identified as files from the project-chosen Springer Nature authoring
  template version 3.1 (December 2024).
- [ ] Publisher redistribution terms have been cleared for the final source
  package; publisher assets are not described as covered by MaskImpute's source
  license.
- [ ] Vancouver-numbered bibliography compiles without missing references.
- [ ] Clean build has no undefined references, overfull critical content, or
  missing figures/tables.
- [ ] Source archive contains the single flattened manuscript, bibliography,
  separate figures, tables, supplement, code/data statement, and checklist.
- [ ] All submitted manuscript, bibliography, table, figure, and supplement
  sources are editable.
- [ ] The cover letter states Genome Biology Methodology fit, the evidence-bound
  advance, relevant policy issues and competing interests, all-author approval,
  and no simultaneous submission.
