# Genome Biology manuscript workspace

The prior NeurIPS draft, generated tables, figures, and associated results are
preserved under `historical/v26_neurips/neurips_paper/` as development evidence.
They are not evidence for the new submission.

The Genome Biology manuscript will be regenerated here only from sealed,
machine-readable publication assets after the method and comparison panel have
been frozen and the one-use evaluation has completed. Author metadata and
declarations remain absent until supplied and approved by the authors.
