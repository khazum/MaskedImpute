# Third-Party Assets and Licenses

This archive contains author-created scripts plus references to third-party datasets and methods cited in the manuscript. Public datasets and external packages are not redistributed here. Users should obtain them from their original sources and follow the corresponding licenses and terms.

Primary external assets/methods cited by the paper include Splatter/Splat, Baron pancreas, Zeisel cortex, PBMC68k/10x Genomics annotations, DCA, scVI/scvi-tools, ALRA, MAGIC, AutoClass, ccImpute, and SAVER. The manuscript bibliography provides the scholarly citations.
